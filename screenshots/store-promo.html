Community hall reopening after refurbishment

The council welcomed residents back to the refurbished hall this week. Regular programming resumes next Monday, including youth sport registrations and the neighbourhood gardeners meet-up.

Booking enquiries can be made through the council website. Parking behind the hall remains limited during peak hours.
